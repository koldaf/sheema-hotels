About Us

Welcome to S-Heema Hotels & Suites

Nestled in the vibrant city of Ilorin, S Heema Hotels & Suites offers a sanctuary of comfort and luxury. Our meticulously designed rooms and suites provide the perfect retreat for both business and leisure travelers.

With our prime location at 10 Ayodele Street behind Rhema Chapel Church Tanke Tipper Garage, we offer easy access to the city's major attractions while maintaining a peaceful atmosphere for our guests.
